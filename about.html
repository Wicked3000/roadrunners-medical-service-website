Who We Are

ROADRUNNERS Medical Consultants is a premier occupational health provider specializing in Fitness-to-Drive Medical Assessment of Transport and Heavy Machinery Workforce in Safety-critical and Safety-sensitive Industries.

Our team of experienced clinicians, psychologists, and health specialists work together to ensure that every worker in safety-critical roles meets the highest standards of medical and psychological fitness.
