Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At ROADRUNNERS Medical Consultants, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal and medical information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide to us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our medical consultancy and assessment services, including:

  • Personal identification (Name, DOB, ID details)
  • Contact information (Email, Phone, Address)
  • Occupational health history and assessment data
  • Employer-provided information for fitness-to-drive certifications

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely for:

  • Conducting medical and psychological fitness assessments
  • Issuing professional medical certifications
  • Communicating with you regarding your appointments or results
  • Meeting legal and regulatory health standards

3. Data Security

We implement strict technical and organizational measures to ensure your data is protected against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Medical records are stored in accordance with industry-standard privacy regulations.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access your records, request corrections, and inquire about how your data is processed at any time.
